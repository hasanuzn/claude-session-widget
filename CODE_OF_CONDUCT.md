# Code of Conduct

Claude Session Widget is a small community project. We want it to be a friendly place for anyone who
wants to use it, report a problem, translate it or improve it — whatever their experience, background,
identity or language.

## What we expect

- **Be respectful and patient.** Assume good intent. Many people here write in a second language.
- **Be constructive.** Critique code and ideas, not people. Explain *why* when you disagree.
- **Be helpful to newcomers.** Every contributor was a first-time contributor once.
- **Respect privacy.** Don't post other people's personal information, and don't share private session
  names, paths or transcripts — your own or anyone else's.
- **Accept decisions gracefully.** Not every idea fits the project; a "no" is about the change, not
  about you.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats or intimidation, in public or in private.
- Discriminatory jokes or language, or sexualized content.
- Personal attacks, trolling or deliberately derailing discussions.
- Publishing someone else's private information without permission.
- Spam, or repeatedly pushing a topic after being asked to stop.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages and reviews —
and to anyone representing the project elsewhere.

## Reporting and enforcement

If you experience or see behavior that breaks this code, contact the maintainer,
[@hasanuzn](https://github.com/hasanuzn), privately via GitHub — for example through the repository's
[private reporting form](https://github.com/hasanuzn/claude-session-widget/security/advisories/new)
(start the title with "Conduct report"). Please don't report conduct issues in a
public thread.

Reports are handled confidentially. Depending on what happened, the maintainer may:

1. ask for the behavior to stop, with a private note explaining why;
2. edit, hide or delete comments, commits, issues or pull requests;
3. temporarily or permanently block someone from the project.

The maintainer will act as fairly and quickly as they can, and will not hold a good-faith report
against the person who made it.
